A concise global overview of major hurricane / typhoon / cyclone / monsoon seasons by region

Summary of Global Peaks: May–November: Northern Hemisphere tropical cyclones. November–April: Southern Hemisphere cyclones and monsoons. June–September: Dominant global wet season due to Northern Hemisphere monsoons.

Tropical cyclone (hurricane / typhoon / cyclone) seasons

Region Local name Main season Peak months
North Atlantic (Caribbean, Gulf of Mexico, US East Coast) Hurricanes June – November August – October
Eastern Pacific (off Mexico) Hurricanes May – November July – September
Western Pacific (Philippines, Japan, Taiwan, China) Typhoons Year-round (main: May – November) July – October
North Indian Ocean (India, Bangladesh, Myanmar) Cyclones April – June & October – December May, November
Southwest Indian Ocean (Madagascar, East Africa) Cyclones November – April January – March
Australian region (northern Australia, PNG) Cyclones November – April February – March
South Pacific (Fiji, Tonga, Samoa) Cyclones November – April January – March

Monsoon seasons

Region Monsoon / Name Main period Notes
South Asia (India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal) Southwest Monsoon June – September Primary wet season for the Indian subcontinent.
Southeast Asia (Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Malaysia) Southwest Monsoon May – October Wet season driven by monsoon winds.
East Asia (China, Korea, Japan) Meiyu / Baiu / Changma June – July Early summer rainy season.
West Africa (Sahel, Nigeria, Ghana) West African Monsoon May – October Wet season due to ITCZ movement.
Northern Australia Wet season / Monsoon November – April Overlaps with cyclone season.
