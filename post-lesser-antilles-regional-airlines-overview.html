1. Air Antilles (Guadeloupe-based) Main Hub: Pointe-à-Pitre (PTP), Guadeloupe Key Destinations: Fort-de-France (Martinique), Saint-Barthélemy (St. Barts), Saint-Martin (Grand Case, French side), Cayenne (French Guiana, occasionally), Santo Domingo (Dominican Republic, seasonal) Notes: Strong focus on French Caribbean connectivity. 2. LIAT (Leeward Islands Air Transport) – Antigua-based Main Hub: V.C. Bird International Airport (ANU), Antigua Key Destinations: Antigua, Barbados, Dominica, Grenada, St. Kitts, St. Lucia, St. Vincent, Tortola Notes: Historically the backbone of Eastern Caribbean travel. Currently restructuring but still operating limited routes. 3. Winair (Windward Islands Airways) (Sint Maarten-based) Main Hub: Princess Juliana International Airport (SXM), Sint Maarten Key Destinations: Saba, St. Eustatius, St. Barthélemy, St. Kitts, Antigua, Tortola (BVI), Dominica Notes: Famous for the world’s shortest commercial runway at Saba. 4. Caribbean Airlines (Trinidad & Tobago-based, but serves Lesser Antilles) Main Hubs: Port of Spain (POS), Trinidad; Piarco International Key Destinations in Lesser Antilles: Grenada, Barbados, St. Lucia, St. Vincent, Antigua Notes: Connects the southern Lesser Antilles with Trinidad and Guyana. 5. SVG Air (St. Vincent & the Grenadines) Main Hub: Argyle International Airport (SVD), St. Vincent Key Destinations: Grenadines (Bequia, Canouan, Mustique, Union Island), Barbados, St. Lucia Notes: Essential for small-island hops in the Grenadines. 6. Mustique Airways (St. Vincent & the Grenadines) Main Hub: St. Vincent Key Destinations: Grenadines (private charters and scheduled flights) Barbados St. Lucia Notes: Popular with luxury travelers heading to Mustique. 7. Divi Divi Air (Curaçao-based, Dutch Caribbean) Main Hub: Curaçao International Airport Key Destinations: Bonaire Aruba Notes: Small commuter airline, vital for Dutch Caribbean links. 8. Z Air / EZ Air (Dutch Caribbean) Main Hubs: Bonaire & Curaçao Key Destinations: Aruba Bonaire Curaçao St. Eustatius Saba Notes: Expanding regional network in the Dutch Caribbean. 9. Air Caraïbes (French Caribbean, Guadeloupe/Martinique) Main Hubs: Pointe-à-Pitre (Guadeloupe), Fort-de-France (Martinique) Key Destinations in Lesser Antilles: Guadeloupe Martinique St. Martin St. Lucia Notes: Also operates long-haul flights to Paris.
