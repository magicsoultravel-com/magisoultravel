lesser antilles inter-island ferry connections

Hub / Island Group Operator(s) Routes / Destinations Notes
French Antilles (Guadeloupe, Dominica, Martinique, St. Lucia) L’Express des Îles Pointe-à-Pitre (Guadeloupe) ↔ Roseau (Dominica) ↔ Fort-de-France (Martinique) ↔ Castries (St. Lucia) High-speed catamarans; backbone of French Antilles ferry travel
Guadeloupe (Les Saintes, Marie-Galante) CTM Deher, Val’Ferry, Express des Îles Pointe-à-Pitre ↔ Les Saintes (Terre-de-Haut); Pointe-à-Pitre ↔ Marie-Galante Frequent daily service; popular day trips
St. Maarten / St. Martin hub Anguilla Ferry, Voyager, Great Bay Express, The Edge, Makana Marigot ↔ Anguilla; Philipsburg ↔ St. Barts; Philipsburg ↔ Saba; Philipsburg ↔ St. Eustatius St. Maarten is the northern ferry hub; very frequent Anguilla service
St. Kitts & Nevis ↔ Sint Maarten Makana Ferry Basseterre (St. Kitts) ↔ Philipsburg (Sint Maarten), sometimes via Statia/Saba Several sailings weekly; ~4–6 hrs; alternative to short flights
St. Kitts ↔ Nevis Sea Bridge, Caribe Queen, MV Mark Twain Basseterre (St. Kitts) ↔ Charlestown (Nevis) Frequent daily commuter ferries; 45 min crossing
St. Vincent & the Grenadines Bequia Express, Admiralty Transport, Jaden Sun, Mustique Co. Kingstown ↔ Bequia, Mustique, Canouan, Mayreau, Union Island Lifeline for Grenadines; multiple daily sailings
Grenada ↔ Carriacou / Petite Martinique Osprey Lines St. George’s (Grenada) ↔ Hillsborough (Carriacou) ↔ Petite Martinique Daily fast ferry; ~90 min crossing
Trinidad & Tobago TTIT (Trinidad & Tobago Inter-Island Transport) Port of Spain (Trinidad) ↔ Scarborough (Tobago) Fast ferries (2.5 hrs) and cargo ferries; very busy route
Dominica ↔ Guadeloupe / Martinique L’Express des Îles Roseau ↔ Pointe-à-Pitre; Roseau ↔ Fort-de-France Key link for Dominica post-hurricane recovery
Virgin Islands (USVI ↔ BVI) Native Son, Road Town Fast Ferry, Smith’s Ferry, Inter-Island Boat Services St. Thomas ↔ Tortola; St. Thomas ↔ Virgin Gorda; St. John ↔ Tortola Frequent commuter ferries; vital for tourism and locals
